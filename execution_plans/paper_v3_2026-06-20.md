# Plan: Address the Review in the Paper and Roadmap

## Summary

Revise the paper to distinguish resolved concerns from remaining limitations, and extend roadmap Tasks 6A–12 for the required diagnostics and experiments. Tasks 1–6 remain unchanged. The main additions are inventory-aware robustness analysis, calibrated residual adequacy checks, lag selection, distributional wind diagnostics, and conservative treatment of nonnegative-boundary identifiability.

## Paper Changes

1. **Time-varying activity**
   - Treat the static-activity concern as resolved by the temporal-basis formulation.
   - Ensure the method and evaluation sections consistently describe coefficient recovery at the selected temporal resolution.

2. **Background basis construction**
   - State that the primary background basis \(Q\) is fixed before fitting and is never selected using the observed pollution response.
   - Identify the rank-4 basis as the primary specification.
   - Present alternative bases only as predeclared sensitivity scenarios.
   - Report how changing \(Q\) affects source visibility, absorption, rank, and ambiguity.

3. **Conservative source grouping**
   - Describe connected-component groups as deterministic, conservative reporting units—not necessarily the globally finest identifiable partition.
   - Explain explicitly that an \(A\!-\!B\!-\!C\) ambiguity chain groups all three sources even when \(A\) and \(C\) are individually distinguishable.
   - Preserve the triggering source pairs and criteria in the reported edge table.

4. **Nonnegativity and boundary identifiability**
   - Clarify that the rank and coherence conditions provide uniform guarantees over the full admissible coefficient set.
   - Acknowledge that a particular coefficient vector on the nonnegative-cone boundary may be locally more identifiable.
   - Do not infer improved identifiability from fitted near-zero coefficients.
   - Allow scientifically justified coefficients to be fixed at zero only through a mask declared before fitting; remove those columns and recompute every diagnostic.
   - State that no tangent-cone or active-set identifiability claim is made.

5. **Lag-window selection**
   - Clarify that changing \(L\) changes the response fingerprints and their temporal support, not the number of observations \(N\).
   - Define a pre-fit candidate grid using physical transport and residence-time bounds.
   - Select the smallest \(L\) satisfying
     \[
     \frac{\|H(L+\Delta)-H(L)\|_F}
          {\max(\|H(L+\Delta)\|_F,\epsilon)}
     \leq \tau_L,
     \qquad \tau_L=10^{-3}.
     \]
   - Require rank, conditioning, and ambiguity-group stability to be reported across the lag sweep.
   - Prohibit selecting \(L\) using the final recovered source coefficients.

6. **Separate transport and inventory uncertainty**
   - Decompose response mismatch conceptually into transport error, inventory error, and their interaction, even if a shared perturbation bound is used in theory.
   - Treat wind and dispersion ensembles as transport uncertainty suitable for uncertainty intervals.
   - Treat alternative source locations, labels, and inventory maps as inventory robustness scenarios—not statistical confidence intervals.
   - State that source-specific conclusions remain conditional on the supplied inventory.

7. **Missing-source and model-adequacy checks**
   - Add a post-fit parametric-bootstrap adequacy test using an externally supplied observation-noise covariance:
     \[
     T_{\mathrm{res}}=r^\top\Sigma_e^{-1}r.
     \]
   - Refit each bootstrap replicate and compare the observed statistic with the null distribution at \(\alpha=0.05\).
   - If no calibrated noise model is supplied, report residual summaries with status `uncalibrated`, without a pass/fail claim.
   - Include sensor-wise, time-wise, and autocorrelation residual summaries.
   - State explicitly:
     - rejection indicates model inadequacy but does not uniquely identify a missing source;
     - non-rejection cannot establish inventory completeness;
     - omitted signals lying in the span of \([H,Q]\) may remain undetectable.

8. **Distributional wind identifiability**
   - Distinguish diagnostics for a realized wind window from prospective network adequacy over a wind distribution.
   - Across historical or simulated wind windows, report:
     - 5th, 50th, and 95th percentiles of \(\sigma_J\);
     - probability of effective/full rank;
     - probability each coefficient is weak;
     - probability each source pair is ambiguous;
     - frequency of each resulting ambiguity component.
   - Present this as an empirical distributional extension, not a new theorem.

9. **Evaluation structure**
   - Split the inventory/transport robustness hypothesis into:
     - H5a: wind and dispersion perturbations;
     - H5b: inventory perturbation and misspecification.
   - Add result placeholders for lag sensitivity, omitted-source adequacy, wind-distribution diagnostics, and background-basis sensitivity.
   - Update the conclusion so all identifiability claims are explicitly conditional on inventory completeness, declared model structure, and observation-noise assumptions.

## Roadmap Changes

### Task 6A — PyTorch consolidation and provenance

- Keep the simulator, projection, diagnostics, and solvers PyTorch-native.
- Attach inventory identifier/hash, transport configuration, wind realization, dispersion settings, observation mask, device, and dtype to response artifacts.
- Introduce explicit provenance tags distinguishing `transport` and `inventory` ensembles.
- Do not modify completed Tasks 1–6.

### Task 7 — Identifiability diagnostics

- Accept `fixed_zero_indices` or an equivalent admissible-coefficient mask.
- Remove masked columns before constructing \(J\), while retaining the original-to-reduced index mapping.
- Add aggregation over wind ensembles with quantiles and event probabilities.
- Retain global uniform diagnostics; do not add tangent-cone optimization.

### Task 8 — Solver and adequacy diagnostics

- Apply the same fixed-zero mask in fitting and diagnostics.
- Add the parametric-bootstrap residual-adequacy interface with:
  - `calibration_status`;
  - \(T_{\mathrm{res}}\);
  - bootstrap quantile and \(p\)-value;
  - \(\alpha\);
  - noise-model provenance.
- Use 1,000 bootstrap replicates for reported experiments.
- Keep transport uncertainty outputs separate from inventory robustness outputs and reject attempts to pool them into one interval.

### Task 9 — Source grouping

- Formalize connected components as conservative report groups.
- Store all trigger edges and diagnostic values.
- Mark outputs with `is_conservative=true`.
- Remove any claim that the partition is always the finest globally identifiable grouping.

### Task 10 — Experiment suite

Add experiments for:

- lag convergence and diagnostic stability;
- preregistered primary and sensitivity \(Q\) specifications;
- separate transport and inventory perturbation suites;
- a residual-visible omitted source outside the fitted span;
- an aligned omitted source demonstrating that residual non-rejection does not prove completeness;
- historical and simulated wind-window ensembles.

### Task 11 — Reporting

Report:

- selected lag and convergence curve;
- coefficient mask and index mapping;
- background-basis specification;
- residual calibration status, statistic, quantile, and \(p\)-value;
- transport uncertainty intervals;
- inventory scenario robustness separately;
- wind-ensemble quantiles and ambiguity probabilities;
- conservative groups and their triggering edges.

### Task 12 — Documentation and reproducibility

- Document the preregistered \(Q\), lag protocol, coefficient mask, inventory version, wind provenance, and noise model.
- Publish schemas for diagnostic, adequacy, ensemble, and grouping artifacts.
- State the conditional interpretation and limitations of every reported certificate.

## Verification and Acceptance Tests

- A fixed-zero mask removes the intended columns and produces consistent reduced/original index mappings.
- Post-fit near-zero coefficients do not alter identifiability diagnostics.
- An \(A\!-\!B\!-\!C\) chain deterministically produces one conservative component with both trigger edges retained.
- Lag selection returns the smallest candidate satisfying the convergence threshold and leaves \(N\) unchanged.
- Transport and inventory ensemble results cannot be pooled.
- Calibrated, correctly specified simulations are not systematically rejected.
- An omitted source outside \(\operatorname{span}[H,Q]\) is detected with increasing signal strength.
- An aligned omitted source demonstrates the documented limitation of residual adequacy.
- Missing noise calibration produces `uncalibrated`, never a model-adequacy pass.
- Wind-ensemble summaries are deterministic under fixed seeds.
- The primary \(Q\) is recoverable from provenance and is never response-selected.
- The paper builds without unresolved references, and terminology matches the roadmap.

## Assumptions and Defaults

- Tasks 1–6 are complete and will not be rewritten.
- Nonnegative-boundary treatment uses the selected conservative clarification; no tangent-cone diagnostic is introduced.
- Default lag convergence threshold is \(10^{-3}\).
- Residual-test significance is \(0.05\), with 1,000 bootstrap refits for paper results.
- Wind summaries use the 5th, 50th, and 95th percentiles.
- Inventory variations are robustness scenarios, not confidence intervals.
- Empirical values remain placeholders until the corresponding roadmap experiments are run.
